# 🎈 Welcome to the AI Preschool Playground!

Imagine you are in preschool, and you want to ask the **Big Storyteller** (our slow, expensive AI) to tell you a story. But what if someone tries to ask for a *scary* story or the teacher's secret cookie passwords? 🍪

Here is how our heroes save the day:
* 🚪 **AgentGateway** is the giant, friendly door to the preschool. Every question has to pass through here first.
* 🐶 **Jev** is AgentGateway’s super-fast Guard Dog. Jev doesn't tell stories, he just sniffs the question and answers "YES" or "NO" really fast!
